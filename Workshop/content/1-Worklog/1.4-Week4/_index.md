### Week 4 Objectives

* Learn the fundamentals of **Application Load Balancer (ALB)**.
* Learn about **Listeners, Target Groups, and Health Checks**.
* Practice configuring an **Application Load Balancer**.
* Learn about **Auto Scaling** and automatically adjusting the number of EC2 instances.
* Learn about **Launch Templates, Auto Scaling Groups, and Scaling Policies**.
* Learn about **High Availability and Fault Tolerance** architectures on AWS.
* Learn about an architecture combining **ALB, EC2, and Auto Scaling**.
